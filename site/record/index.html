The record syntax

It breaks up a record with two channels in one file: one that plays for anyone, and one sealed with a key. It has 3 element types and 6 elements, and a record described in it is one choice of elements from each type.

Instances

  • Zariia: a record simulator: upload two tracks and download one record, the second track sealed inside it with a key (zariia.org)

Element types and elements

open channel holds one

The track anyone hears. It plays whole in any player, with no key.

sealed channel holds one

A second track carried in the same file and locked with a key. Anyone can see that it is there; with the key it plays in sync with the open channel.

class holds one

What the sealed channel is, beside the open one.

the counter-voice
another take of the same moment
the room
what else was audible: talk, error, the street
the refusal
what was cut, and why
the reply
a second musician answering, recorded later
the score
the instruction that produced the result
open throughout
there is no sealed channel

Left out

What either channel says or sounds like: the syntax names what each channel is, never its content, and the sealed channel's content stays keyed. The class open throughout belongs to the syntax, but a record made from two tracks cannot have it, so the simulator does not offer it.
